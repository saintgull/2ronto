In the meantime, here are some quick facts about Toronto:

Canada's Largest City

With a population of nearly 3 million (6 million in the Greater Toronto Area), Toronto is Canada's economic engine and cultural capital.

The World's Most Multicultural City

Over 50% of Toronto's residents were born outside of Canada, with over 200 ethnic groups and 140 languages spoken.

Safe and Stable

Consistently ranked as one of the safest major cities in North America with excellent public services and infrastructure.
