Event Discovery Platforms

Toronto has a thriving events scene with something happening every day. Here are the best resources to find events that match your interests:

Toronto Events Live

A comprehensive calendar of events happening across Toronto, from concerts and festivals to workshops and community gatherings.

Toronto Event Generator Newsletter

A curated newsletter by Misha Glouberman featuring unique, weird, and high-participation events. Perfect for finding non-mainstream activities like philosophy discussions at bars, silent book clubs, and community experiments.

Luma Discover

Browse and discover events across Toronto on Luma's platform. Great for finding tech meetups, professional networking events, and creative workshops.

Community Resources

Finding your community is essential to feeling at home in Toronto. These resources will help you connect with like-minded people:

Toronto Communities Directory

An official directory of community organizations, cultural groups, and neighborhood associations across Toronto. Find communities based on your interests, culture, or neighborhood.

Tips for Getting Connected

  • Start with events in your neighborhood to meet locals
  • Join recurring events or series to build relationships over time
  • Consider volunteering - it's a great way to meet people while giving back
  • Toronto Public Library branches often host free events and workshops
  • Look for Facebook groups specific to your interests or neighborhood
  • Check community center bulletin boards for local happenings
