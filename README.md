# All About Toronto (2ronto)

A comprehensive guide for people moving to and living in Toronto, Canada.

## About This Project

This website serves as a practical resource for newcomers to Toronto, inspired by the successful "All About Berlin" guide but tailored specifically for Toronto's unique character and needs.

## Why This Site Exists

Moving to a new city can be overwhelming. While Toronto is known for being one of the world's most livable and diverse cities, navigating its systems, finding community, and settling in can still be challenging for newcomers.

This guide aims to:

- **Connect people to Toronto's vibrant communities** - With over 200 ethnic groups and 140 languages spoken, finding your community is key to feeling at home
- **Simplify the settlement process** - Clear, actionable information about administrative tasks, from getting OHIP to understanding the TTC
- **Highlight Toronto's event scene** - Curated resources to discover everything from major festivals to quirky neighborhood gatherings
- **Provide practical neighborhood insights** - Understanding Toronto's diverse neighborhoods and the Greater Toronto Area

## Key Features

- **Events & Communities Directory** - Curated list of platforms to find events and connect with communities
- **Life in the City Guide** - Public transportation, neighborhood overviews, and GTA cities
- **Administrative Resources** - Essential government services, healthcare, banking, and education links
- **Why Toronto** - (Coming soon) Comprehensive overview of what makes Toronto an attractive destination

## Design Philosophy

The site follows a minimalist design approach with:
- Clean, easy-to-scan layouts
- Mobile-responsive design
- Focus on practical, actionable information
- Direct links to official resources
